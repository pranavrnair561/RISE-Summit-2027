# RISE Summit 2027 – Event Poster and Teaser

Promo package for **RISE Summit 2027**, a fictional clean-tech and AI conference.
It has a poster and a 12-second teaser video. Both use a rising sun in amber and teal on night blue, with glitch effects and a countdown.

## Poster
![RISE Summit 2027 poster](Poster_4_5_1x.png)

- Format: 4:5 portrait, 1080 × 1350 px
- Glitch title with RGB-split layers and shifted slices
- Retro striped sun, countdown boxes (days, hours, minutes, seconds), date and venue block
- Fonts: Sora Bold (display) and Inter (body)

## Teaser video
[▶ Watch the teaser](rise-summit-teaser.mp4)

- Length: 12 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, no audio
- Glitch transitions on every countdown number

| Time | Scene |
|------|-------|
| 0–1 s | "Signal incoming" flicker |
| 1–6 s | Countdown from 5 to 1, with a glitch burst on each number |
| 6–7 s | Heavy glitch on 00:00, then a white flash |
| 7–10 s | Sun ring rises, RISE title glitches in, tagline fades up |
| 10–12 s | Date, venue and registration card |

## Notes
The date (March 12–14, 2027), website and venue are placeholders for a fictional event.

## Tools
Poster designed in a Claude design canvas. Teaser rendered with Python (Pillow and FFmpeg). The layout can be rebuilt in Illustrator, Photoshop and After Effects.
